I think that something that would add a very interesting element is that we would populate the map with a random number of starter towns, and a random number or knights or companies. They would be able to recruit soldiers from towns, and then would be motivated by certain elements to go out and fight. within those armies that are recruited - there is a chance for a soldier to become a named entity if they satisfy some sort of requirement or defeat an existing hero or slay a certain number of foes or something and could split off as a new hero for the faction or strike it out as a neutral faction taking a certain number of soldiers with them from the original army depending on some factors. then we could 'follow' on king and later in history we would see a whole new band of warriors roaming around. Also I like the idea of 'aging' or settling down in a spot and creating new towns or kingdoms based on the army size, or the gold they collected that would then be a place that heroes could restock or rearm for battle. depending on existing faction alliances or rivals that town might be part of an existing kingdom or something. Again, this is basically all emergent based on a lot of different factors.
